Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding ordering, express shipping, and product authenticity.

How do I know my medicines are 100% authentic?
STACKLY sources products directly from FDA-approved manufacturers and certified state distributors. Every batch is cataloged with batch numbers and expiry dates verified by licensed pharmacists.
What are your shipping times and delivery costs?
We offer FREE Express Shipping on all orders over $50. Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST are dispatched the same day. Standard delivery takes 1-3 business days.
Can I speak directly to a pharmacist about my order?
Yes! Our licensed clinical pharmacists are available 24/7 via phone or our live chat widget to answer any dosage or drug interaction questions.
What is your return policy for healthcare items?
For health and safety compliance, unopened wellness items and medical devices can be returned within 30 days. Contact support to initiate a return shipping label.
How do I upload or verify a prescription?
You can upload a clear photo or PDF of your valid prescription during checkout or email it directly to rx@stackly.com with your order number.
