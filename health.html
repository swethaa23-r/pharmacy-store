Ask Our Clinical Pharmacists

Expert answers to common patient questions regarding vitamins & medications.

Vitamin Q&A

"Can I take Vitamin D3 with Calcium?"

Yes! Vitamin D3 significantly enhances intestinal absorption of Calcium into bone tissue. Taking them together is clinically recommended.

Storage Q&A

"Should probiotics be kept refrigerated?"

Refrigeration preserves live CFUs (Colony Forming Units) longer, though shelf-stable micro-encapsulated strains remain effective below 25°C.

Dosage Q&A

"What is the best time to take Magnesium?"

Magnesium Glycinate is best taken 30–60 minutes before bedtime as it relaxes neuromuscular tension and promotes deep restorative sleep.
